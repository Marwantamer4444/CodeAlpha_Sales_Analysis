# CodeAlpha Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship**.

The main objective of this project is to analyze the **Superstore Sales Dataset** using Python. The project focuses only on the completed tasks: **Exploratory Data Analysis** and **Data Visualization**.

Through this project, sales data was explored, cleaned, analyzed, and visualized to extract useful business insights related to sales performance, profit, categories, regions, products, discounts, and monthly trends.

## Main Insights

- Technology is the strongest category in both sales and profit.
- The West region achieved the highest sales and profit.
- Phones, Chairs, and Storage are among the top-performing sub-categories by sales.
- Copiers generated the highest profit among sub-categories.
- Tables and Bookcases caused losses and need business review.
- High discounts can reduce profit and sometimes lead to negative profit.
- Monthly sales and profit trends show changes in business performance over time.
- The company should focus on profitable categories and review weak-performing sub-categories.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Focus more on the Technology category because it has the highest sales and profit.
2. Increase marketing efforts in the West region because it is the best-performing region.
3. Review discount strategies because high discounts can reduce profit.
4. Investigate loss-making sub-categories such as Tables and Bookcases.
5. Promote high-performing products and sub-categories to improve revenue.
6. Use monthly sales trends to support inventory planning and marketing decisions.
